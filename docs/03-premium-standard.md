# Premium Standard

Premium is the operating standard of the method.

It does not mean expensive, glossy, animated, or maximal. It means the product behaves in a way that earns user trust.

## Definition

Premium means:

- the main action is immediately understandable;
- the system's status is truthful;
- uncertainty is visible;
- defaults are high quality;
- advanced control exists without polluting the first screen;
- errors explain what happened and what can be done;
- costs are visible or at least qualified;
- risky actions are protected;
- user journeys are testable end to end;
- the product avoids unpleasant surprises.

## The Two-World Standard

The method aims for a synthesis:

- **Apple-like clarity**: coherence, smoothness, low cognitive load, polished UX, obvious primary action.
- **Windows-like depth**: configuration, openness, inspectability, user control, advanced settings, and power-user workflows.

The goal is neither closed elegance nor chaotic power. The goal is simple first, deep when needed.

## Premium Failure Modes

A product is not premium if:

- it looks refined but cannot complete a real journey;
- it hides costs until after action;
- it shows simulated data as real;
- it uses "done" when gates have not passed;
- it forces end users to understand logs or pipelines;
- it buries the main action under configuration;
- it gives experts no way to inspect or control behavior;
- it creates irreversible changes without approval.

## Premium Checklist

Use this before claiming a product slice is premium:

- Can a beginner identify the primary action quickly?
- Can an expert inspect what happened?
- Are mock, inferred, unknown, and real data clearly distinguished?
- Is the core journey tested end to end?
- Are cost and resource implications understood?
- Are red actions blocked by approval?
- Are advanced controls available without overwhelming the default path?
- Does the UI reveal truth rather than confidence theater?

## Evidence Requirement

Do not call something premium because it is intended to be premium. Call it premium only when there is evidence: tested journeys, clear UI, reliable behavior, truthful status, and known limits.
