# Hermes Agency OS Case Study

This case study is sanitized and public-safe. It does not include private credentials, private file paths, unpublished client material, personal data, raw logs, or sensitive operational details.

## Context

Hermes Agency OS is used here as an example of an agentic operating workflow where autonomous workers support repeatable product, editorial, or operational tasks.

The important lesson is not the specific tool. The important lesson is how a workflow becomes reliable enough to operate.

## From Workflow To Golden Path

An early workflow may start as a sequence of prompts and manual checks. It becomes a golden path when:

- the inputs are explicit;
- expected artifacts are known;
- status is truthful;
- failure modes are named;
- the worker has checkpoints;
- reports are consistent;
- human approval points are clear.

## Reliability Became Necessary

Autonomy exposed problems that were easy to miss in manual runs:

- partial completion looked like success;
- skipped checks were not always visible;
- quota limits could interrupt work quietly;
- UI scaffolds could look like product progress;
- logs existed but did not answer product questions.

## Reusable Lesson

A durable autonomous workflow needs wrappers, checkpoints, observable status, quota policy, and final reports that can be challenged.

This applies beyond Hermes: Codex sessions, CLI agents, chatbots with tools, and scheduled automations all need the same governance pattern.
